World Consumer Rights Day

World Consumer Rights Day: Purpose, Importance & How To Celebrate

World Consumer Rights Day is celebrated on March 15th every year to raise awareness about consumer rights and promote consumer protection.


FAQ

What is World Consumer Rights Day?

World Consumer Rights Day is an annual global observance day celebrated on March 15th to raise awareness about consumer rights and protection.The day was first observed in 1983 and is coordinated by Consumers International, a global federation of consumer organizations.

What is the purpose of consumer day?

The purpose of Consumer Day, also known as World Consumer Rights Day, is to promote consumer rights and protection.

The day aims to raise awareness about consumer issues, empower consumers to make informed choices, and encourage businesses to operate with greater transparency and accountability.

The day highlights the importance of protecting these rights and promoting fair and ethical business practices.

What are the main responsibilities of a consumer?

Consumers have several responsibilities that are important for creating a fair and equitable marketplace where businesses can operate with transparency and accountability. Here are some of the main responsibilities of a consumer:

  • Being informed: 
  • Exercising caution
  • Providing feedback
  • Advocating for their rights

Similarly inform offers if product and services of companies are of substandard. Consumer must insist on getting complete information on the quality, quantity, utility, price etc. of the product or services.
